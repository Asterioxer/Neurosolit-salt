Clinical Rationale & Threat Models

Why three architectures instead of one general-purpose chatbot

1. Schizoid / SZPD Profile

Threat: Disclosure & Engulfment Anxiety. Data leaving the device at all triggers vulnerability panic.

Solution: Architecture 1 (Liminal-Space) runs 100% in-browser on client WebGPU/WASM with zero server round-trip.

Offline WebGPU

2. PDA Autism Profile

Threat: Perceived Demand. Imperative commands, questions, and unsolicited wellness advice trigger nervous system fight/freeze.

Solution: Architecture 2 (Declarative-Ally) uses Linda K. Murphy's BERMED declarative language to strip demands.

ZeroGPU A10G

3. Chronic Withdrawal Profile

Threat: Social Fatigue & Unpredictability. Fear of being trapped in conversational spirals.

Solution: Architecture 3 (NeuroACT) grounds pacing in focused special interests, with verbal shutdown circuit breakers.

BGE-Small RAG

Clinical Foundations:

  • Linda K. Murphy: BERMED Declarative Language Handbook (removing demands and interrogatives).
  • Richard G. Erskine: Attunement to Silence & Schizoid Process (safeguarding autonomy and emotional perimeter).
  • Johan Pahnke: NeuroACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy adapted for neurodivergent populations).
  • Dora Raymaker & Christina Nicolaidis: Characterizing Autistic Burnout and Inertia.
